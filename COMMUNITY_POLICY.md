# Ember CoreUI Free Community Policy

Ember CoreUI is distributed by Starlight Unit Studios as a free source-available community project.

For the official Ember CoreUI project, the current policy is:

- no purchase price
- no paid license tier
- no subscription fee
- no paid white-label program
- no donation mechanism tied to Ember CoreUI
- no advertising or sponsorship monetization inside Ember CoreUI
- no paid official support package
- no license phone-home, installation reporting, or user-count telemetry

Internal use by individuals, communities, businesses, educational institutions, and other organizations remains free when the Ember CoreUI Community Source License and required branding are respected.

Branding exceptions are not automatic. If Starlight Unit Studios grants an individual written exception under the current policy, it is handled without an Ember CoreUI license fee. The project is not obligated to approve an exception.

This policy describes the official project's present non-monetized operation. It is not a promise concerning unrelated Starlight Unit Studios works and does not replace `LICENSE.de.md` or `LICENSE.md`. Any future policy change would apply prospectively, be documented transparently, and would not withdraw rights already granted for earlier copies.
